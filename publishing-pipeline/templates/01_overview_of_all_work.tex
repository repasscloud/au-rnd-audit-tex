\section{Purpose of this document}
\RDSectionIntro{This is the program-level overview document. It should read as the authoritative summary of the full year of work, while the independent experiment runs, working papers, time records, infrastructure worksheets, and git evidence appendices operate as supporting background records.}

\RDAuditNote{This document should not be a marketing narrative. It should state the actual technical problem, why existing knowledge was insufficient, what was attempted, what evidence exists, and how the program-level claim maps to underlying records.}

\section{Program profile}
\RDMetaTable

\subsection{Product or platform}
Describe the software product, subsystem, service, or platform being developed. Identify the commercial context without overstating certainty, novelty, or business outcomes.

\subsection{Program objective}
State the overall technical objective. Focus on the engineering capability sought, not merely the feature list.

\subsection{Commercial context}
Summarise the market requirement, delivery constraints, performance expectations, and why the work mattered commercially.

\section{Technical state of the art before the program}
Describe the baseline technical knowledge and known approaches available before commencing the relevant activities.

\begin{RDChecklist}
\item Existing architectures, frameworks, or vendor solutions reviewed.
\item Constraints of conventional implementation patterns.
\item Prior internal versions, prototypes, or failed attempts.
\item Third-party limits that prevented straightforward implementation.
\item Relevant standards, protocol assumptions, or documented platform limits.
\end{RDChecklist}

\section{Audit readiness checklist}
\begin{RDChecklist}
\item Each claimed project is linked to a genuine technical uncertainty.
\item Each uncertainty is linked to one or more experiment runs.
\item Each experiment run records method, conditions, observations, and outcome.
\item Commits, tickets, and engineering artefacts are linked to the relevant period.
\item Salary and contractor apportionment is supported by timesheets or documented allocation logic.
\item Infrastructure allocation is supported by a reproducible method.
\item The annual claim pack references all supporting schedules and appendices.
\end{RDChecklist}
