\section{Purpose}
\RDSectionIntro{This register maps the annual claim to the underlying technical work. It should allow a reviewer to move from each claimed activity to the relevant uncertainty, experiment evidence, engineering artefacts, personnel effort, and expenditure schedules.}

\section{Instructions}
\begin{RDChecklist}
\item Use one row per claimed activity grouping or sub-stream.
\item Separate core activity logic from supporting activity logic.
\item Record the basis for inclusion, not just the activity label.
\item Reference the exact notebook IDs, repositories, and expenditure schedules used to support the row.
\end{RDChecklist}

\section{Annual claim logic summary}
\subsection{Core activity narrative}
Summarise the principal experimental streams and the technical questions they addressed.

\subsection{Supporting activity narrative}
Summarise the directly related enabling activities, their boundaries, and any apportionment principles applied.

\subsection{Boundary notes}
Explain how routine engineering, maintenance, implementation-only work, customer configuration, sales support, or general production operations were identified and excluded or reduced.
