\section{Purpose}
\RDSectionIntro{This pack is intended to capture weekly activity allocation where employees or contractors perform both eligible and non-eligible work. The aim is to preserve a contemporaneous basis for apportionment rather than relying solely on year-end estimates.}

\section{Completion rules}
\begin{RDChecklist}
\item Complete weekly or at the close of each sprint.
\item Allocate hours to meaningful activity categories, not vague labels.
\item Tie R\&D-coded work back to a project ID or run ID.
\item Keep explanatory comments where a week contains mixed work.
\item Ensure totals reconcile to actual worked time or paid time basis used for apportionment.
\end{RDChecklist}
